The short version: Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, I may earn a commission — at no extra cost to you. I only recommend things I genuinely believe in.

Full Disclosure

iMarketing Club participates in affiliate and referral programmes. This means some links on this website may be affiliate or referral links. When you click one and make a purchase or sign up for a paid service, I may receive a commission from the vendor. This is how I fund the running of this site and continue producing free content.

Current Affiliate Relationships

Affiliate or referral relationships on this site may include the iMarketing Club community on Skool and other platforms and vendors from time to time. Where a paid upgrade or course is available inside the community, I may earn a referral fee if you purchase through a link on this site. This is always disclosed clearly.

My Commitment to You

Earning a commission never influences what I recommend. Here's my personal commitment:

  • I only recommend products I have personally used, closely reviewed, or have strong reason to believe are genuinely useful
  • If I think something is poor value or poorly made, I won't promote it — regardless of commission rates
  • My opinions and editorial content are my own and are not influenced by affiliate relationships
  • I will always clearly disclose affiliate relationships where they exist

Your Cost Is the Same

Using my affiliate links does not increase the price you pay. In some cases, affiliate links may grant access to exclusive discounts or bonuses that wouldn't be available going directly to the vendor's site.
