Who I Am

Not a Guru.
Just Someone Who Does This.

I’m Jay Salton. I’ve been in digital marketing and affiliate marketing for over a decade, based out of Queensland, Australia. I’ve run campaigns, tested tools, built email lists, written sales pages, promoted offers, and made plenty of expensive mistakes along the way.

What I’m not is a “seven-figure guru.” I’m not going to show you an income screenshot and promise you can replicate it in 30 days if you buy my course. That’s not what this is. What I can offer is honest, practitioner-level perspective — the kind that comes from actually doing the work, not from watching other people do it.

iMarketing Club started as a place to share what I was testing and learning. It’s grown into something more useful: a community for people who want a real system for building an online business — not theory, not hype, not recycled advice.

What I’ve Learned

Eleven Years In —
Here’s What I Know for Sure

The number one thing that separates people who make consistent money online from people who don’t isn’t intelligence or luck. It’s having a proven business model and sticking with it long enough to make it work.

I’ve watched people with less skill and less effort build real online income — because they found a system that worked and followed it. I’ve also watched smart, hard-working people spin their wheels for years because they kept jumping from one strategy to the next without ever going deep on one thing.

“The people who make it online aren’t the smartest ones. They’re the ones who stopped looking for something better and started getting really good at the one thing that was working.”

That’s why iMarketing Club is built the way it is. Not a hundred different strategies. One clear system. One community. One place to go when you have questions.

Why AI?

AI Changed What’s Possible.
Especially for Beginners.

When I started in digital marketing, creating content, writing copy, and building an audience took an enormous amount of time. The barrier to entry was high — not because the strategies were complicated, but because execution was slow.

AI has changed that equation significantly. Content that used to take a day can take an hour. Research that used to take a week can take minutes. Copy that used to require expensive freelancers can be drafted in seconds and refined from there.

That doesn’t mean AI does the work for you. It means the leverage is higher. You still need a clear system, a real offer, and the discipline to follow through. But if you combine a proven business model with AI-powered execution, you can move a lot faster than someone who was doing this five years ago.

That’s the premise behind the AI Digital Mastery Training inside the community. Not “let AI do everything.” Just: here’s how to use AI intelligently inside a business model that works.
