Welcome to the New iMarketing Club —
AI, Digital Courses, and What’s Changed

By Jay Salton — Founder, iMarketing Club

iMarketing Club has been around for a while. If you’ve been here from the beginning, you’ll know it started as a blog — a place to share honest tool reviews, affiliate marketing guides, and straight-talk on what actually works in digital marketing.

That hasn’t gone away. But something has changed, and I want to be clear about what it is and why.

Why I Built a Community

Blogs are useful. But a blog on its own has a limitation: it’s one-way. You read something, you have a question, and there’s nowhere to ask it. You try to implement something, you hit a wall, and there’s no one to talk it through with. That friction is why most people who read good advice still don’t act on it.

A community fixes that. So I built one.

iMarketing Club now has a home on Skool — a platform I chose specifically because it keeps courses and community in the same place, without the noise and algorithm manipulation of Facebook groups. The community is free to join. The training inside is free. And what I’ve put in there is genuinely the most useful material I’ve found for people who want to build an online income in 2026.

The AI Digital Mastery Training

The centrepiece of the community is a free 4-part video training series called the AI Digital Mastery Training.

I want to be direct about something: I didn’t create this training. I give my community access to it because I believe it’s one of the clearest, most actionable business models I’ve come across for selling digital courses and make-money-online products using AI.

The system this training is built on has generated over $13,000,000 in online sales over 13 years. That’s not a claim I make lightly — and it’s not theoretical. It’s a documented, real-world business model with a track record.

What makes it relevant right now is AI. The model has always worked. What AI does is compress the timeline — content that used to take weeks can be produced in hours, traffic that used to require a big budget can be generated more efficiently with the right tools, and systems that used to require a team can now be run by a single person.

“AI hasn’t changed the fundamentals of building an online business. It’s changed the speed and leverage at which those fundamentals can be applied.”

What Else is Inside

Beyond the 4-part training, the community has:

  • An active forum where you can ask questions, share progress, and get feedback from other members
  • A paid course (optional) containing a 90% done-for-you white label system — pre-built course content, sales pages, and email sequences you can brand and sell as your own
  • Ongoing updates as the model evolves and new AI tools emerge

The forum alone is worth joining. If you’ve ever tried to build an online business in isolation — bouncing between YouTube videos and blog posts with no one to ask when things don’t make sense — you’ll understand the value of having a focused, relevant community to plug into.

What Hasn’t Changed

The blog is still here. I’ll still be writing about AI, digital marketing, and what’s working — the same way I always have: directly, without hype, and with the assumption that you’re a capable adult who wants honest information.

What has changed is that the community gives the blog a practical next step. Read an article, get interested in the model, join the community and start applying it. That loop didn’t exist before. Now it does.
