The Best AI Tools for Selling
‘Make Money Online’ Digital Courses in 2026

By Jay Salton — Founder, iMarketing Club

There are a lot of AI tools. Most of them are either redundant, overpriced, or trying to do too much at once. For digital course sellers in the make-money-online and online business space, the toolset that actually matters is narrower than the marketing suggests.

I’ve spent time testing what actually moves the needle for selling digital courses — not just what the productivity influencers say is revolutionary. Here’s the honest breakdown.

What You Actually Need (And What You Don’t)

Before the tool list: a framing point. The biggest mistake I see digital course sellers make is over-investing in tools and under-investing in execution. You don’t need 12 AI subscriptions. You need three or four things that work, and the discipline to use them consistently.

The core workflow for selling digital courses involves: creating content to attract an audience, capturing leads, nurturing subscribers via email, and converting those subscribers into buyers. Every tool in your stack should directly serve one of those four functions.

Content Creation: Where AI Has the Biggest Impact

AI Writing Assistants (Claude, ChatGPT)

For digital course sellers, an AI writing assistant is non-negotiable. You need to produce blog posts, social captions, email newsletters, sales page copy, and ad copy — constantly. Doing all of that manually is simply not viable at the volume that builds an audience.

The workflow: brief the AI clearly (audience, goal, tone), use the output as a first draft, then rewrite for your voice. The AI gives you structure and a starting point; you give it personality and accuracy.

Claude is the AI writing assistant I use most. It handles long-form content, sales copy, and email sequences well, and produces fewer generic platitudes than some alternatives.

AI Video Scripts

If you’re using YouTube, TikTok, or Reels to drive traffic — and you should be — AI can generate scripts for short-form and long-form video. Give it the topic, the target viewer, and the call to action, and it will produce a usable draft in seconds. Video content without a script is a waste of camera time.

AI Image Generation (for thumbnails and social graphics)

Midjourney and similar tools can produce professional-quality social graphics and YouTube thumbnails without a graphic designer. For make-money-online content, high-contrast thumbnails with clear text overlays consistently outperform polished but vague images. AI generation is good enough for this.

Traffic Generation: Amplifying What You Create

SEO Research with AI

AI has made keyword and topic research significantly faster. You can prompt an AI to identify the questions your audience is asking, map out a content calendar that addresses those questions, and identify the search intent behind different keywords — all without a paid SEO tool subscription.

The caveat: AI doesn’t have real-time search data. Use it for ideation and content planning, not for precise keyword volume numbers.

AI-Assisted Ad Copy

If you’re running paid traffic to your opt-in, AI is useful for generating ad copy variants to test. The make-money-online space has specific compliance constraints on paid platforms — be careful about income claims in your AI-generated copy before it goes live.

Email: The Revenue Engine

AI Email Sequence Writing

The most time-consuming part of setting up an email funnel is writing the sequences. AI can generate welcome sequences, nurture sequences, and promotional sequences at a level that’s genuinely usable with editing. The structure AI produces is usually sound; the tone needs adjustment to feel human.

For make-money-online offers, a 7-email welcome sequence that builds trust before making an offer converts better than leading with the pitch in email one. AI understands this if you brief it correctly.

Sales: Converting Subscribers to Buyers

AI Sales Page Copy

Sales pages for digital courses follow a well-established structure: headline, problem identification, solution introduction, proof, offer details, objection handling, call to action. AI can produce a draft of this entire page in minutes. It won’t be perfect — it will need editing, and it won’t include your real testimonials or specific proof — but the bones will be there.

“The best AI tool stack is the smallest one that gets the job done. More tools means more time managing tools and less time building the business.”

The Minimum Viable AI Stack

If I were starting today and wanted to keep things simple:

  1. One AI writing assistant for content, copy, and email drafts
  2. One email marketing platform to host your list and automate sequences
  3. One funnel/landing page tool to capture leads and sell the course
  4. One traffic channel to distribute content consistently

That’s four tools. Anything beyond that is optional until the fundamentals are working.
