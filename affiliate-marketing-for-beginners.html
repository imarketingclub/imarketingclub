How to Use AI to Start
Affiliate Marketing From Scratch in 2026

By Jay Salton — Founder, iMarketing Club

Most beginner guides to affiliate marketing are written by people who haven’t run an active campaign in years. They talk about “finding your passion” and “building trust” without giving you anything concrete enough to act on. This isn’t that.

I’ve been doing this for over a decade. And in the last two years, AI has changed the starting position for beginners more than anything else I’ve seen in that time. The content that used to take days takes hours. The research that used to require expensive tools can be done in minutes. The sales copy that used to need a freelancer can be drafted in seconds.

If you’re starting from scratch in 2026, you have an advantage that didn’t exist in 2022. Here’s how to use it.

What Affiliate Marketing Actually Is (The Short Version)

You promote someone else’s product. When someone buys through your link, you earn a commission. You don’t handle the product, the customer service, or the fulfilment. Your job is to find buyers and send them to the right offer.

That’s it. The complexity comes in the execution — specifically, in building the systems that get you in front of buyers consistently and profitably.

Why Digital Courses Are the Right Product Category for Beginners

There are thousands of things you can promote as an affiliate. Physical products, software, services, memberships. But for beginners using AI, digital courses in the make-money-online and personal development space are the strongest starting point, for three reasons:

  • High commissions. Digital products have low overhead, so vendors can offer 30%–75% commission rates. Physical product affiliate programmes typically pay 3%–10%.
  • AI-compatible content. Writing about how to make money online, productivity, or skill-building is something AI handles well. The content category lends itself to AI-assisted creation.
  • Done-for-you options. White label digital courses mean you can have a product to sell — or a product to use as a free lead magnet — without spending months creating one.

The AI-Powered Workflow for Beginners

Here’s a practical breakdown of where AI fits in each stage of starting an affiliate business:

1. Niche Research

Use AI to map out the competitive landscape before you commit to a direction. Ask it to identify sub-niches within make-money-online, outline the main problems people in that niche have, and list the types of products they buy. You’ll get a usable market map in minutes rather than days.

2. Content Creation

This is where AI has the biggest impact for beginners. You can produce blog posts, email sequences, social media content, and YouTube scripts at a volume that would have required a full-time content team five years ago.

“The bottleneck for most affiliate marketers isn’t knowledge. It’s output. AI removes that bottleneck.”

The key is to use AI as a starting point, not a final product. Draft with AI, then edit for your voice. The content needs to be genuinely useful — AI can generate volume, but it can’t replace judgment about what’s actually helpful to the reader.

3. Email Sequences

An email list is still the highest-ROI asset in affiliate marketing. Use AI to write welcome sequences, educational nurture emails, and promotional sequences for your offers. Give AI clear context — who the reader is, what they’re struggling with, what you want them to do — and you’ll get drafts worth editing.

4. Sales Funnels

AI can draft opt-in page copy, thank you page copy, and basic sales page structure. Combined with a white label product, this can compress your setup time from months to weeks.

5. Traffic

AI helps here too — for writing SEO-optimised blog content, for generating social media post ideas, for crafting ad copy. But AI can’t replace the work of actually distributing content consistently. The discipline still has to come from you.

The Biggest Mistake Beginners Make

They spend too much time in research and tool evaluation mode, and not enough time building and distributing. AI makes it easier to stay in research mode — you can generate an endless stream of plans, frameworks, and analyses without ever putting anything live.

The move that separates people who build businesses from people who research them is: ship something. Get a page live. Send an email. Publish a post. The feedback from real activity is worth more than any amount of planning.

Where to Start

If I were starting from scratch today, I’d do the following:

  1. Pick a product to promote — a digital course in the make-money-online or AI skills space
  2. Build a simple opt-in page with an AI-written lead magnet (or access a done-for-you one)
  3. Set up a basic email sequence using AI — 5–7 emails introducing the problem and the solution
  4. Start producing content (blog, social, or both) using AI as a drafting assistant
  5. Drive traffic to the opt-in through consistent content distribution

That’s the loop. Everything else is either a refinement of that loop or a distraction from it.
