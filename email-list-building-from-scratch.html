How to Build and Monetise an Email List
for Your Digital Course Business Using AI

By Jay Salton — Founder, iMarketing Club

Your Instagram following can disappear overnight. Your TikTok account can get banned. Your YouTube channel can be demonetised. Your email list can’t be taken from you by an algorithm.

After more than a decade in digital marketing, the thing I keep coming back to is this: the businesses that survive platform changes, algorithm updates, and market shifts are the ones with strong email lists. Everything else is rented land. Your email list is the asset you actually own.

And here’s what’s changed: AI has made building one dramatically faster and cheaper than it was even two years ago.

Why Email Matters More for Digital Course Sellers

If you’re selling digital courses in the make-money-online or personal development space, email isn’t just useful — it’s foundational. Here’s why:

  • The average return on email marketing is among the highest of any digital channel
  • Digital course purchases often require multiple touchpoints — email sequences can do that work automatically
  • You can build a relationship with subscribers over time, increasing trust before you ask them to buy
  • Once the infrastructure is set up, it runs without your ongoing manual input

Most people who fail at selling digital courses fail not because their product is bad, but because they have no mechanism for reaching the same prospect more than once. Email solves that problem.

Step 1: Create a Lead Magnet That Actually Converts

Nobody gives you their email address for nothing. You need something worth trading for it. In the digital course space, the highest-converting lead magnets are specific, immediately useful, and connected to what you’re eventually selling.

Good examples:

  • A free mini-training or video series (3–5 minutes of genuinely useful content)
  • A short PDF guide solving a specific problem your audience has
  • A framework or checklist that saves the reader significant time

AI can write the content of your lead magnet faster than you can do it manually. Give it a clear brief — who the reader is, what specific problem it should solve, what format it should take — and use the output as a draft to refine into something that genuinely represents you.

Step 2: Build a Simple Opt-In Page

Your opt-in page has one job: get the visitor to hand over their email address. Keep it simple. A strong headline, three to five bullet points about what they’re getting, a form, and a submit button. Nothing else.

AI can write this page in minutes. Use it to generate three or four headline variants, then pick the one that’s most specific and benefit-focused. Specificity converts better than cleverness.

Step 3: Write an Email Welcome Sequence With AI

The moment someone subscribes is the highest point of their engagement with you. The welcome sequence — the first five to seven emails after someone opts in — is your best opportunity to set expectations, deliver value, and begin the relationship that leads to a sale.

AI can draft this sequence quickly. A basic structure:

  1. Email 1: Deliver the lead magnet, introduce yourself briefly, tell them what to expect
  2. Email 2: Share something useful — a tip, a lesson, a resource — with no ask
  3. Email 3: Tell your story — why you do this, what you’ve learned
  4. Email 4: Address a common problem or objection your audience has
  5. Email 5: Introduce your offer — softly, framed around how it solves the problem you’ve been addressing
  6. Email 6: Address objections and reinforce the value of the offer
  7. Email 7: Final call to action or transition to a broadcast email cadence

Generate this with AI, then rewrite in your voice. The structure AI gives you is usually sound — the tone is what needs adjustment.

“Your email list is not just a channel for promotions. It’s a relationship. The more genuinely useful your emails are, the higher your conversion rate when you do make an offer.”

Step 4: Drive Traffic to the Opt-In

A perfectly written opt-in page with no traffic is worthless. Use AI to produce a consistent stream of content that drives people to it:

  • SEO-optimised blog posts targeting questions your audience is searching for
  • Social media content with a clear call to action pointing at the opt-in
  • Short-form video scripts (for YouTube Shorts, Reels, or TikTok) about the problem your lead magnet solves

AI handles the drafting. You handle the distribution. The discipline of showing up consistently is still a human job.

Step 5: Monetise With Relevant Offers

Once you have subscribers coming in and a working welcome sequence, the monetisation layer is straightforward. Promote digital courses that solve the problems your list has. For the make-money-online space, there’s no shortage of high-quality products with strong affiliate programmes and generous commissions.

The key is relevance. An email list built around AI marketing should receive offers about AI marketing tools and courses. A list built around passive income strategies should receive offers around that. Mismatched offers kill engagement fast.
